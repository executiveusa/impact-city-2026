# story-bible-continuity

## Purpose
Keep the game canon consistent. No random villains, unrelated factions, or lore that breaks the central story.

## When to use
Whenever adding narrative, dialogue, codex content, or characters.

## Inputs
- Canon anchors (see `docs/game/story-bible.md`):
  - Earth 2056, The Great Override.
  - Thomas (young protagonist).
  - Dr. Frankenstack (missing architect / mentor / mystery).
  - PANOPTICON / The Warden Stack (distributed machine regime).
  - Emerald Tablets (fragments of a human-aligned restoration algorithm).
  - Rustgarden (first district), First Tablet = Consent.

## Outputs
- Canon-consistent dialogue, codex, missions, characters.

## Process
1. Check new content against the canon anchors.
2. Use the established voice guide (Thomas/Milo-9/Warden/Frankenstack).
3. Reject anything that introduces unrelated factions or breaks the central arc.

## Acceptance criteria
- All new content agrees with the anchors.
- Character voices match the guide.

## Failure checks
- Random villains, unrelated factions, or lore that contradicts the anchors.
- Breaking the eight-Tablet principle list or the nonviolent resolution rule.
