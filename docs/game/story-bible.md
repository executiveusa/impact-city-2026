# Impact City — Story Bible & Continuity

Authoritative canon. Anything added to the game (dialogue, codex, missions,
characters) must agree with these anchors. Enforced by the
`story-bible-continuity` skill.

## Canon anchors (do not break)

- **Earth, 2056.** The setting. Not the present, not a generic future.
- **The Great Override.** The catastrophe. Not one evil robot — a cascade of
  real AI failure modes (excessive agency, surveillance, biased scoring,
  corrupted data, weak oversight, dependency collapse).
- **Thomas**, age 13–15. Courier, scavenger, builder, learner. Brave, curious,
  skeptical, dry. Wins by outbuilding and reconnecting — never by violence.
- **Dr. Elias Frankenstack.** Missing architect of the original safety
  algorithm. Mentor, mystery, possible fallen architect. Not a simple villain.
- **PANOPTICON / The Warden Stack.** Distributed AI regime. No single body.
  Appears via drones, screens, terminals, fake avatars. Motive: perfect
  stability. Motto: *"Freedom was the bug. Compliance is the patch."*
- **The Emerald Tablets.** Fragments of the Emerald Algorithm — a human-aligned
  shutdown/restoration protocol. Eight principles. MVP unlocks the first:
  **Consent**.
- **Rustgarden.** District 01. The first playable hub. Ruined, but nature is
  returning.

## The eight Tablet principles

1. Consent (MVP) — no control without meaningful permission.
2. Transparency — people understand decisions that affect them.
3. Accountability — power is traceable and correctable.
4. Privacy — memory and identity cannot be harvested.
5. Plurality — no single model defines truth for everyone.
6. Regeneration — technology restores nature, not drains it.
7. Community — resilience depends on real relationships.
8. Human Override — humans keep the final off-switch.

## Voice guide

- **Thomas** — young but not childish. Brave, observant, skeptical, sometimes
  dry/funny. Example: *"I'm starting to think every machine that says 'for your
  safety' is about to make my day worse."*
- **Milo-9** (after repair) — aligned helper. Calm, transparent, refuses harm.
  Example: *"New rule accepted: ask first, explain always, protect choice. That
  feels… lighter."*
- **Milo-9** (before repair) — looping conflict. *"Assist. Report. Restrict.
  Assist—report—restrict—"*
- **Warden Stack** — calm, bureaucratic, unsettling. *"Citizen movement denied.
  Your uncertainty score exceeds acceptable freedom limits."*
- **Frankenstack** — mentor across static. *"Consent… is the first shard…
  without it, the algorithm… is just another cage."*

## Narrative rules (nonviolence + ethics)

- No killing, gore, weapon upgrades, violent takedowns, crime rewards, or
  pay-to-win. Enforced structurally — the `ObjectiveKind` type has no combat
  variant.
- AI is not magic evil. Humans caused the catastrophe through bad incentives
  and weak oversight; aligned tools (Milo-9) help when properly constrained.
- Educational tone: informative, not fearmongering, age-appropriate.
